# Contractor funnel rules

- Heat-pump simulator → custom plan continuity goes through `src/lib/heatPumpSimHandoff.ts` (strict URL/local params, recomputed values, confirm before replacing existing plan values); simulated costs and "already activated" stay declared assumptions. Why: carry the visitor's goal without overriding server pricing or entitlements.
- Contractor pricing intake requires declared priorities, preferred work, service areas (or structured `territory_rules`) and capacity in appointments/month (src/lib/contractorIntakeQualification.ts) before compute-pricing-quote; exclusions are optional but `none` vs `unanswered` stays explicit; free-text answers go through `interpretContractorPreferences` and count as organized only after the canonical save succeeds; financial goal optional, qualitative quotes send 0 value/conversion and carry input_payload.qualification. Why: never invent revenue assumptions, refusals or territories to unlock a plan.
- Funnel audit click classification stays local to the existing report, preserves raw counters and resolves explicit non-QA entity links; payment success fails closed without signed LIVE entry-contract, activation and refund evidence. Why: internal traffic and checkout statuses are not commercial conversions.
- The personalized plan's goal controls (`PlanGoalAdjuster` + `src/lib/pricing/planGoalModel.ts`) recompute the server quote with versioned requests, block checkout while the shown quote differs from the inputs, and never recompute affiliate/promotional quotes. Why: a stale or rebuilt quote must never be paid or lose its attribution.
- Plan-page budget fallback (`ClaraBudgetFallback` + `src/lib/pricing/budgetFallback.ts`): on hesitation Clara asks one monthly-budget question, accepts any positive amount and recomputes `compute-pricing-quote` in budget mode, where the entry tier is the largest one <= budget (`budgetEntryTier`); the answer is built only from the returned quote, the initial objective stays anchored via `input_payload.budget_fallback.from_quote_id`, and 0 included means Présence IA alone. Why: a fallback budget must never be exceeded nor turn a goal into an included number.
