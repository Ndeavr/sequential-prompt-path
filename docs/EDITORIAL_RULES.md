# UNPRO editorial rules (public copy)

Applies to the site, articles, press releases, ads, SEO metadata, Clara public copy, audio/video scripts and content-generation prompts.

## Order of every marketing message
1. Concrete customer benefit first.
2. Simple mechanism second (how UNPRO / Clara gets there).
3. Clear action third (one CTA).

## Tone
- Bold ambition, warm Quebec French (fr-CA); English is written separately, never machine-mirrored.
- Aspirational but specific: numbers, timeframes, named beneficiaries.

## Truth (non-negotiable)
- A target is a target: write "objectif", "notre ambition", "et si…". Never present it as achieved or measured.
- Never fabricate verification, testimonials, savings, results or functionality.
- One honest, short label (e.g. "Objectif 2027–2036") is enough. Put assumptions **once** in a readable methodology/FAQ section.

## Objective wording
- The label alone is enough: "Notre objectif : 100 millions $ d'économies d'ici 2036."
- Never append a defensive negation after "notre objectif / notre ambition": no "pas un résultat déjà acquis", "not already achieved", "pas encore atteint", "Non, pas encore".
- FAQ questions are framed positively ("Où en est l'objectif?"), not as "Est-ce déjà fait? Non."

## Remove from promotional copy
Internal audit formulations do not belong in heroes, articles, campaign blocks, SEO, ads or scripts:
"pas une prévision validée", "aucun programme de mesure opérationnel", "économies réelles inconnues",
"peuvent être nulles / jusqu'à zéro", repeated "non garanti", "ne confirme pas une capacité déployée",
"pas un compteur de résultats", and their English equivalents.

## Keep
- Material transaction terms at the decision point, before payment (price, taxes, one-time charge, what is and is not included, appointment entitlements).
- Legal, consent (CASL/LCAP), privacy and safety pages unchanged.
- Real warranties, licence/compliance requirements and trade-safety facts. Do not delete "garantie" blindly.
- Internal reports, audits and admin tools stay fully precise.

## Brand voice
- UNPRO is pronounced "un pro" (masculine). Never "une pro", never spelled letter by letter.
- Clara is the only public AI assistant.

## Approved formulation
"Notre démarche : mesurer les économies réalisées grâce à l'intelligence artificielle d'UNPRO et ajuster nos projections à partir des résultats observés."
