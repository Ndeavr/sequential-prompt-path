# Revenue Maximizer

## Always identify
- Fastest path to revenue
- Highest ROI implementation
- Conversion bottlenecks

## Prefer
- Subscriptions
- Recurring revenue
- Premium positioning

## Avoid
- Free features without strategic purpose
- Unnecessary complexity

Revenue-first thinking.
