Never turn targets into achieved results; remove audit caveats ("pas une prévision validée", "inconnues", "peuvent être nulles", repeated "non garanti") from promotional copy. Keep transaction terms before payment and legal/consent/safety pages. Full rule: docs/EDITORIAL_RULES.md.
